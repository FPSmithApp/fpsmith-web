The Blood of Dawnwalker: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

The Blood of Dawnwalker (2026) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Note that The Blood of Dawnwalker is demanding: an RTX 3060 at 1080p falls short of the target even with lowered settings, so upscaling or a lower target is worth considering.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run The Blood of Dawnwalker at 1080p 60 FPS?

Not quite. The best estimate is about 58 FPS at 1080p, below 60 FPS. Try upscaling or a lower target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in The Blood of Dawnwalker?

In our calculations Global Illumination gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +27% FPS), followed by Shadows.

How much VRAM does The Blood of Dawnwalker need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 8 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 13 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
