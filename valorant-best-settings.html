Valorant: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Valorant (2020) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Because Valorant is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Detail Quality: up to +7% FPS
  • Material Quality: up to +6% FPS
  • Cast Shadows: up to +4% FPS
  • Bloom: up to +3% FPS

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Valorant at 1080p 240 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 405 FPS at 1080p, above the 240 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Valorant?

In our calculations Detail Quality gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +7% FPS), followed by Material Quality.

How much VRAM does Valorant need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 2.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 3 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.
