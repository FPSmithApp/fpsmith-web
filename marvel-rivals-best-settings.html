Marvel Rivals: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Marvel Rivals (2024) gives you a long list of graphics options, but only a few of them decide whether you hit your frame-rate target. Because Marvel Rivals is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p. At very high targets Marvel Rivals is limited by the processor as much as the graphics card: an RTX 3060 at 1080p lands at about 98 FPS rather than 144, which is still smooth for most players.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Marvel Rivals at 1080p 240 FPS?

Not quite. The best estimate is about 121 FPS at 1080p, below 240 FPS. Try upscaling or a lower target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Marvel Rivals?

In our calculations Global Illumination gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +36% FPS), followed by Shadow Detail.

How much VRAM does Marvel Rivals need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 5.6 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 7 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.
