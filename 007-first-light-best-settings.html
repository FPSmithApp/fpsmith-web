007 First Light: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting 007 First Light (2026) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run 007 First Light at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 62 FPS at 1080p, above the 60 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in 007 First Light?

In our calculations Global Illumination Quality gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +22% FPS), followed by Volumetric Fog Quality.

How much VRAM does 007 First Light need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.2 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 11.7 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
