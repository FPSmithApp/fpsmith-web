Hunt: Showdown 1896: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting Hunt: Showdown 1896 (2024) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Because Hunt: Showdown 1896 is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p. At very high targets Hunt: Showdown 1896 is limited by the processor as much as the graphics card: an RTX 3060 at 1080p lands at about 113 FPS rather than 144, which is still smooth for most players.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Hunt: Showdown 1896?
