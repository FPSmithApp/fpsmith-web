Forza Horizon 5: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting Forza Horizon 5 (2021) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Good news: Forza Horizon 5 is light enough that even an RTX 3060 at 1080p can run it at maximum settings. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Forza Horizon 5 at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 76 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

How much VRAM does Forza Horizon 5 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 6.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 10.4 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
