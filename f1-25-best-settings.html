F1 25: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

F1 25 (2025) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Good news: F1 25 is light enough that even an RTX 3060 at 1080p can run it at maximum settings. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run F1 25 at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 80 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

How much VRAM does F1 25 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.2 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 11.7 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
