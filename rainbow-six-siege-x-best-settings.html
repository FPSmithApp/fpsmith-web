Rainbow Six Siege X: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Rainbow Six Siege X (2025) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Because Rainbow Six Siege X is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Ambient Occlusion: up to +7% FPS
  • Shading Quality: up to +6% FPS
  • Reflection Quality: up to +5% FPS
  • Shadow Quality: up to +3% FPS

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Rainbow Six Siege X at 1080p 240 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 253 FPS at 1080p, above the 240 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Rainbow Six Siege X?

In our calculations Ambient Occlusion gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +7% FPS), followed by Shading Quality.

How much VRAM does Rainbow Six Siege X need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 5 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.
