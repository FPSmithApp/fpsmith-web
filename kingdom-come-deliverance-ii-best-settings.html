Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Lighting: up to +4% FPS
  • Global Illumination: up to +4% FPS
  • Shader Quality: up to +4% FPS
  • Object Quality: up to +3% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 3 settings: Object Quality (Ultra → High), Shadows (Ultra → High), Vegetation Detail (Ultra → High). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Kingdom Come: Deliverance II at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 62 FPS at 1080p, above the 60 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Kingdom Come: Deliverance II?

In our calculations Lighting gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +4% FPS), followed by Global Illumination.

How much VRAM does Kingdom Come: Deliverance II need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 6.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 10.4 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
