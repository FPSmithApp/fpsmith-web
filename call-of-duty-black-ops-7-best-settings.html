Call of Duty: Black Ops 7: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 (2025) gives you a long list of graphics options, but only a few of them decide whether you hit your frame-rate target. Because Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p. At very high targets Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 is limited by the processor as much as the graphics card: an RTX 3060 at 1080p lands at about 141 FPS rather than 144, which is still smooth for most players.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 at 1080p 240 FPS?

Not quite. The best estimate is about 174 FPS at 1080p, below 240 FPS. Try upscaling or a lower target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Call of Duty: Black Ops 7?

In our calculations Shadow Quality gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +10% FPS), followed by Detail Quality.

How much VRAM does Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.2 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 9 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.
