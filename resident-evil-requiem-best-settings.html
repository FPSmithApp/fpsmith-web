Resident Evil Requiem: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting Resident Evil Requiem (2026) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Good news: Resident Evil Requiem is light enough that even an RTX 3060 at 1080p can run it at maximum settings. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 1 setting: Volumetric Fog Resolution (High → Normal). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Resident Evil Requiem at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 71 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Resident Evil Requiem?

In our calculations Volumetric Fog Resolution gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +6% FPS).

How much VRAM does Resident Evil Requiem need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 8 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 13 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
