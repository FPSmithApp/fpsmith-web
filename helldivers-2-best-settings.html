Helldivers 2: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Volumetric Fog Quality: up to +4% FPS
  • Object Detail Quality: up to +3% FPS
  • Particle Quality: up to +1% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 3 settings: Object Detail Quality (High → Medium), Particle Quality (Ultra → High), Volumetric Fog Quality (Ultra → Medium). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Helldivers 2 at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 69 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Helldivers 2?

In our calculations Volumetric Fog Quality gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +4% FPS), followed by Object Detail Quality.

How much VRAM does Helldivers 2 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 6.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 10.4 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
