The Witcher 3 Remastered: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting The Witcher 3 Remastered (2026) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run The Witcher 3 Remastered at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 69 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

Which setting costs the most FPS in The Witcher 3 Remastered?

In our calculations Hairworks gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +9% FPS).

How much VRAM does The Witcher 3 Remastered need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.2 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 11.7 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
