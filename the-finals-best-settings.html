The Finals: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

The Finals (2023) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Because The Finals is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Global Illumination: up to +18% FPS
  • View Distance: up to +13% FPS
  • Shadows: up to +12% FPS
  • Foliage: up to +7% FPS

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run The Finals at 1080p 240 FPS?

Not quite. The best estimate is about 186 FPS at 1080p, below 240 FPS. Try upscaling or a lower target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in The Finals?

In our calculations Global Illumination gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +18% FPS), followed by View Distance.

How much VRAM does The Finals need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 4.8 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 6 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.

How we calculate: FPSmith estimates frame rates from independent benchmark data (Hardware Unboxed, TechSpot and others) for each graphics card and processor, then picks the best-looking combination of settings that still meets the target.
