Ghost of Tsushima: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Ghost of Tsushima (2024) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Good news: Ghost of Tsushima is light enough that even an RTX 3060 at 1080p can run it at maximum settings.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Ghost of Tsushima at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 73 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

How much VRAM does Ghost of Tsushima need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 6.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 10.4 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
