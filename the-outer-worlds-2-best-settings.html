The Outer Worlds 2: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

The Outer Worlds 2 (2025) can look very different depending on a handful of settings, and maxing everything is rarely the smartest choice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Global Illumination: up to +27% FPS
  • Shadows: up to +18% FPS
  • Foliage: up to +8% FPS
  • Reflections: up to +7% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 5 settings: View Distance (Cinematic → High), Anti-Aliasing (Cinematic → Epic), Reflections (Medium → Low), Post Processing (High → Low), Foliage (Medium → Low). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run The Outer Worlds 2 at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 62 FPS at 1080p, above the 60 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in The Outer Worlds 2?

In our calculations Global Illumination gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +27% FPS), followed by Shadows.

How much VRAM does The Outer Worlds 2 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 8 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 13 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
