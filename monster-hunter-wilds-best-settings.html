Monster Hunter Wilds: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting Monster Hunter Wilds (2025) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Below are the best-looking settings that still hold 60 FPS on four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p up to an RX 9070 XT at 4K, in the same order as the in-game menu. Ray tracing is left off in these presets; turn it on only if you have frame-rate headroom.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Texture Quality: up to +29% FPS
  • Shadow Quality: up to +11% FPS
  • Grass/Tree Quality: up to +9% FPS
  • Volumetric Fog Quality: up to +9% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 4 settings: Mesh Quality (Medium → Low), Wind Simulation Quality (High → Low), View Distance (Medium → Low), Ambient Occlusion (High → Medium). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Monster Hunter Wilds at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 62 FPS at 1080p, above the 60 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Monster Hunter Wilds?

In our calculations Texture Quality gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +29% FPS), followed by Shadow Quality.

How much VRAM does Monster Hunter Wilds need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 15.6 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
