Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • View Distance / Level of Detail: up to +3% FPS
  • Volumetric Fog & Clouds: up to +2% FPS
  • Reflections: up to +2% FPS
  • Foliage: up to +2% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 5 settings: Volumetric Fog & Clouds (Ultra → High), Reflections (Ultra → High), View Distance / Level of Detail (Ultra → High), Foliage (Ultra → High), Crowds / Physics (High → Medium). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Valheim at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes, easily. The estimate is about 65 FPS at 1080p even with every setting on maximum.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Valheim?

In our calculations View Distance / Level of Detail gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +3% FPS), followed by Volumetric Fog & Clouds.

How much VRAM does Valheim need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 6.5 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
