Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Global Illumination: up to +24% FPS
  • Shadows: up to +14% FPS
  • Effects: up to +7% FPS
  • Reflections: up to +4% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 1 setting: Global Illumination (Medium → Low). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Directive 8020 at 1080p 60 FPS?

Yes. With the settings in this guide the estimate is about 62 FPS at 1080p, above the 60 FPS target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Directive 8020?

In our calculations Global Illumination gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +24% FPS), followed by Shadows.

How much VRAM does Directive 8020 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 7.2 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 11.7 GB at 4K on an RX 9070 XT.
