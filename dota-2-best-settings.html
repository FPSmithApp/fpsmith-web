Dota 2: best graphics settings

Updated: October 8, 2026

Getting Dota 2 (2013) to run smoothly is mostly about knowing which settings cost the most frames and which ones you will actually notice. Because Dota 2 is a competitive game, the settings below favour high, stable frame rates and clear visibility over eye candy. We calculated them for four common PCs, from an RTX 3060 at 1080p to an RX 9070 XT at 1440p.

Which settings to lower first

These settings give the biggest frame-rate gains when you lower them:

  • Ambient Creatures: up to +14% FPS
  • Shadow Quality: up to +8% FPS
  • Effects Quality: up to +6% FPS
  • Ambient Occlusion: up to +4% FPS

Compared with the RX 9070 XT build, the RTX 3060 build lowers 2 settings: Effects Quality (Low → High), Anti-Aliasing (Off → On). Everything else can stay where it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can the RTX 4060 run Dota 2 at 1080p 240 FPS?

Not quite. The best estimate is about 197 FPS at 1080p, below 240 FPS. Try upscaling or a lower target.

Which setting costs the most FPS in Dota 2?

In our calculations Ambient Creatures gives the biggest single gain when lowered (up to +14% FPS), followed by Shadow Quality.

How much VRAM does Dota 2 need?

With the settings in this guide it uses about 2.4 GB at 1080p on an RTX 4060 and about 3 GB at 1440p on an RX 9070 XT.
